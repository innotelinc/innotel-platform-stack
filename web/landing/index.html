V1 · Enterprise Architecture Bundle

Fourteen platforms.
Six golden layers..

The canonical Innotel Platform Stack: strict single-responsibility architecture where Cerulean runs the shared identity and secrets plane — Authentik at auth.cerulean.innotel.us (one login for every platform), Infisical at secrets.cerulean.innotel.us — and owns trust; ONYX owns storage, Magnate owns revenue, NPM Edge owns the edge, and every other platform is a business function that consumes them. No platform duplicates another's responsibility, and any login goes through Cerulean.

The layers of the ecosystem.

Six foundation layers, seven business platforms, and the agent layer on top — each with its own repo and stack doc.

🔑

Foundation layers

Cerulean (Auth & Trust) hosts Authentik (auth.cerulean.innotel.us) and Infisical (secrets.cerulean.innotel.us); ONYX (StorageOps), Magnate (RevenueOps), NPM Edge (EdgeOps). Every platform's auth.<platform>.innotel.us alias fronts the same Cerulean Authentik — SSO everywhere, one identity.

🏢

Business platforms

Monarch (MediaOps), Zeus (VoiceOps), Oasis (MailOps), Signara (DocumentOps), Rizz Aura (CommunityOps), AuthenIQ (LearningOps).

🤖

Agent layer

Capstone (AgentOps) — voice AI agents over Zeus telephony.
